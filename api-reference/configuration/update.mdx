## Verification presets

`verification_preset` sets which documents the Teal webapp asks your applicants for, and when.
Clients start on `default`.

When a `PATCH /configuration` request selects a different preset, the preset's defaults below are
applied to each of these fields that the request does not send. You can then change any of them, in
the same request or later. `default` has no defaults: switching back to it changes no other field.
Presets never restrict what the API accepts.

| Preset | Payroll connections | HMRC | Payslips | P45 | P60 | Universal Credit | Bank statements | `affordability_mode` | `required_documents` | Never asked for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `default` | - | - | - | - | - | - | - | - | - | - |
| `payday` | on | off | on | off | off | off | off | `off` | `[]` | bank statements, P45, P60, Universal Credit |
| `guarantor` | on | off | on | off | off | on | on | `ons_then_bank` | `[]` | P60 |
| `prime-loan` | on | on | on | off | off | off | on | `ons_then_bank` | `[]` | P45, P60, Universal Credit |
| `auto-finance` | on | on | on | on | off | off | on | `ons_then_bank` | `[]` | P60, Universal Credit |
| `mortgage` | on | on | on | on | on | off | on | `bank` | `["bank_statements"]` | Universal Credit |
| `second-charge` | on | on | on | on | on | off | on | `bank` | `["bank_statements"]` | Universal Credit |
| `lettings` | on | off | on | off | off | on | off | `off` | `[]` | bank statements, P60 |
| `recruitment` | on | off | off | off | off | off | off | `off` | `[]` | payslips, bank statements, P45, P60, Universal Credit |
| `gambling-light` | on | off | on | off | off | off | on | `ons` | `[]` | P45, P60, Universal Credit |
| `gambling-vulnerability` | on | off | on | off | off | off | on | `bank` | `[]` | P45, P60, Universal Credit |

The columns map to `payroll_connections_enabled`, `hmrc_enabled`, `payslip_upload_enabled`,
`p45_scan_enabled`, `p60_scan_enabled`, `universal_credit_scan_enabled` and `bank_statement_enabled`.
"Never asked for" lists the documents the webapp does not ask for under that preset, even when you
switch them on. Users can still upload them through the API when their flags allow it.

Bank statements can be a source on their own. Under every preset, an applicant whose only source is
bank statements (`bank_statement_enabled` on, with payroll connections, HMRC and payslip upload off) is
led to a bank statements tile on the connect screen, and a user with only `bank_statement_enabled` on
can be created. `gambling-light` and `gambling-vulnerability` also offer that tile to any applicant
whose payroll connection is off or failed and who has no income yet, even when HMRC or payslip upload is
on. The tile is a way to connect, so it is never required and has no skip alternative, even when
`bank_statements` is in `required_documents`. The preset no longer needs `bank_statement_enabled`: with
bank statements off, the tile is simply not offered.

## Validation

The request is refused with a 400 when the resulting configuration is inconsistent, and nothing is
saved. The most common cases are switching a flag off while something still needs it:

- a document in `required_documents` needs its flag on, e.g.
  `[required_documents] bank_statements is required but bank_statement_enabled is off`;
- `affordability_mode` `bank` or `ons_then_bank` needs `bank_statement_enabled`.

Remove the requirement (or change the mode or preset) before, or in the same request as, switching
the flag off.

---
title: 'Retrieve a specific document'
openapi: 'GET /documents/{document_id}'
---

`file_contents` is the Base-64 encoded PDF. Payslip documents include `trust_score`, `trust_analysis`, and `pdf_metadata`. Bank statement documents leave those fields null. See [Doc Scan](/doc-scan).
